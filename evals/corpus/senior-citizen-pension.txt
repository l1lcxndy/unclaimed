Senior Citizen Pension

The Senior Citizen Pension pays 1000 per month to older people with low household income. People aged 80 or older receive 1500 per month.

Who can apply: people aged 60 or older whose total household income is below 100000 per year.

<<<PAGE>>>

Documents required: age proof (birth certificate or identity card showing date of birth), household income certificate, bank account details.

Who cannot apply: people under 60; households with income of 100000 per year or more; people already receiving a government service pension.

How to apply: apply at the local social welfare office.

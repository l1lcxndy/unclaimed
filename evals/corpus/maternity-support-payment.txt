Maternity Support Payment

The Maternity Support Payment gives 5000 to pregnant women and new mothers, paid in three installments: at pregnancy registration, after the first antenatal checkup, and after the child's birth is registered.

Who can apply: pregnant women and mothers who have given birth in the last 12 months, aged 19 or older, for their first or second live birth.

<<<PAGE>>>

Documents required: pregnancy registration card, identity card, bank account details, and the child's birth certificate for the final installment.

Who cannot apply: women under 19; third or later births; women employed in government service who receive paid maternity leave.

How to apply: register the pregnancy at a health centre within 150 days.

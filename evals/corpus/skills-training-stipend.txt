Skills Training Stipend

The Skills Training Stipend offers free job-skills courses (for example electrician, plumber, tailoring, computer operator) lasting 3 to 6 months, with a stipend of 1500 per month during training.

Who can apply: unemployed people aged 18 to 35 who have completed at least 8 years of schooling.

<<<PAGE>>>

Documents required: identity card, school certificate showing at least 8 years of schooling, bank account details.

Who cannot apply: people currently in full-time employment; people over 35.

How to apply: enrol at any registered skills training centre.

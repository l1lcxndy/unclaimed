Family Health Cover

Family Health Cover gives free hospital insurance of 500000 per family per year for hospital treatment, with cashless care at listed hospitals.

Who can apply: households with a total income below 120000 per year. All family members are covered, with no limit on family size or age.

<<<PAGE>>>

Documents required: family identity card or list of household members, identity card of the head of household, household income certificate.

Who cannot apply: households with income of 120000 per year or more.

How to apply: check eligibility and get the health card at any listed hospital or health centre.

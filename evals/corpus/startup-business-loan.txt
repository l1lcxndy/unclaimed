Startup Business Loan

The Startup Business Loan helps first-time entrepreneurs start a new small business. Loans of up to 1000000 are available, with no collateral required up to 500000. The government pays a subsidy of 25 percent of the project cost, or 35 percent for women and applicants from rural areas.

Who can apply: first-time entrepreneurs aged 18 to 45 starting a new manufacturing, trade or service business.

<<<PAGE>>>

Documents required: business plan, identity card, address proof, and quotations for any equipment to be bought.

Who cannot apply: people over 45 or under 18; people who already own or run a business; people who have received a government-subsidised business loan before.

How to apply: submit the online application; selected applicants attend a short entrepreneurship training before the loan is released.

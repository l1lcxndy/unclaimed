Disability Allowance

The Disability Allowance pays 1200 per month to adults with a significant disability.

Who can apply: people aged 18 or older with a disability of 40 percent or more, as certified by a government medical board.

<<<PAGE>>>

Documents required: disability certificate from a government medical board, identity card, bank account details.

Who cannot apply: people with a certified disability below 40 percent; people under 18.

How to apply: apply at the social welfare office with the medical board certificate.

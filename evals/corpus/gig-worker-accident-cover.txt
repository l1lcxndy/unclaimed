Gig Worker Accident Cover

Gig Worker Accident Cover gives free accident insurance to registered gig and platform workers, such as delivery riders, rideshare drivers and app-based home service workers.

Benefit: 200000 in case of accidental death or permanent total disability, and 100000 in case of permanent partial disability. There is no premium for the worker.

Who can apply: gig and platform workers aged 18 to 60 who are registered on the national worker registry.

Documents required: proof of platform work (app profile screenshot or earnings statement), identity card, nominee details.

Who cannot apply: workers under 18 or over 60; workers already covered by an employer group accident policy.

How to apply: register on the worker registry portal. Cover starts from the date of registration.

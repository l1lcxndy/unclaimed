Rural Housing Grant

The Rural Housing Grant gives 120000 to rural households to build a permanent house, paid in three installments as construction progresses.

Who can apply: households living in rural areas that do not own a permanent house, including families living in mud or thatched houses or temporary shelters.

<<<PAGE>>>

Documents required: identity card, proof of land ownership or a land allotment letter, bank account details.

Who cannot apply: households that already own a permanent brick or concrete house anywhere; households in urban areas.

How to apply: households are selected through the village council list; contact the village council office.

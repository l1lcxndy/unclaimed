Street Vendor Working Capital Loan

The Street Vendor Working Capital Loan gives street vendors a first working capital loan of 10000, repayable in 12 months. Vendors who repay on time can get a second loan of 20000. An interest subsidy of 7 percent per year is credited for timely repayment.

Who can apply: street vendors selling from carts, stalls or the roadside.

<<<PAGE>>>

Documents required: vending certificate, or a letter of recommendation from the local town authority if no certificate has been issued; identity card; bank account details.

Who cannot apply: shop owners with a fixed commercial premises.

How to apply: apply through any bank branch or the online portal.

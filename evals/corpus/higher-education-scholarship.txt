Higher Education Scholarship

The Higher Education Scholarship pays college and university tuition of up to 50000 per year, plus a maintenance allowance of 10000 per year.

Who can apply: students enrolled in a college or university degree course whose total family income is below 250000 per year and who scored at least 60 percent in their last qualifying exam.

<<<PAGE>>>

Documents required: admission letter or college ID, mark sheet of the last qualifying exam, family income certificate, bank account details.

Who cannot apply: students with family income of 250000 or more; students who scored below 60 percent; students already receiving another government scholarship.

How to apply: apply on the scholarship portal before the annual deadline.

Farm Income Support

Farm Income Support gives farmer families direct income support of 6000 per year, paid in three equal installments of 2000 into the family's bank account.

Who can apply: farmer families that own cultivable farmland of up to 5 acres in total. A farmer family means a husband, wife and their children under 18. The land must be recorded in the family's name in the land records.

<<<PAGE>>>

Documents required: land ownership record, identity card of the applicant, bank account details linked to the identity card.

Who cannot apply: families owning more than 5 acres of cultivable land; families where any member pays income tax; serving or retired government employees; institutional landholders. Tenant farmers who do not own land are not covered.

How to apply: register at the local agriculture office or the online portal. Installments are paid every four months after verification.

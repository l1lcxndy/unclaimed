Child Nutrition Grant

The Child Nutrition Grant pays a monthly allowance of 500 to the mother of each child under 6 years of age, to support food and regular health checkups.

Who can apply: mothers of children aged 0 to 6 years. The grant is paid until the child turns 6.

<<<PAGE>>>

Documents required: child's birth certificate, mother's identity card, child's immunisation card.

Who cannot apply: mothers of children aged 6 or older. The child must attend at least two health checkups per year for payments to continue.

How to apply: apply at the nearest community health centre.
